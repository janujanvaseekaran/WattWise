⚡ WattWise — Smart DC Power & Energy Monitor

An ESP32-based embedded system that measures DC voltage, current, power, and accumulated energy consumption in real time.

The Challenge: Many electronic devices don't provide accessible real-time power and energy consumption information.

The Solution: WattWise combines an INA219 sensor, ESP32 microcontroller, and SSD1306 OLED to provide live electrical measurements.

Technologies: ESP32 · C++ · I²C · INA219 · SSD1306 · Arduino IDE · Altium Designer

Project Status: Working breadboard prototype validated; PCB design in progress.
